FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas do you deliver to?

We currently deliver to all major cities and surrounding areas within a 25km radius. For corporate and event catering, we provide nationwide coverage.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We offer vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free options. Our chefs can customize any dish to meet your specific dietary needs.

How far in advance should I book catering?

We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for small events and 4-6 weeks for larger gatherings. Last-minute bookings may be available based on capacity.

Can I cancel or modify my meal subscription?

Yes, you can modify or cancel your subscription at any time with at least 48 hours notice before your next delivery. No cancellation fees apply.

What food safety certifications do you have?

We are FSSAI certified and follow ISO 22000 food safety management standards. Our kitchen is regularly audited for hygiene and quality compliance.
